ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Official information about the least official department.

WELCOME TO THE DEPARTMENT

The Overthinking Department™️ is a completely unofficial organisation dedicated to those thoughts that appear out of nowhere, stay far too long, and somehow become an entire cinematic experience inside your head.

🧠

WHAT IS THIS?

The Overthinking Department™️ is a fictional department created for people whose brains refuse to stop at one simple thought.

🔍

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to investigate random thoughts, imaginary scenarios, old memories, future possibilities and every tiny detail that suddenly feels extremely important.

📜

DEPARTMENT RULES

There are absolutely no official rules. However, thinking about something that happened five years ago at 2 AM is considered completely normal here.

🪶

OUR SPECIALITY

We specialise in analysing conversations, situations, possibilities, memories and the mysterious meaning behind a simple "okay."

⚡

WHY DOES IT EXIST?

Nobody really knows. It probably started with one random thought and somehow turned into an entire department.

🌙

WHO RUNS IT?

The Department is operated by imagination, curiosity, memories, late-night thoughts and approximately 73 ideas arriving at the same time.

OFFICIAL DEPARTMENT NOTICE ⚡

This department is completely unofficial, unnecessarily dramatic, and has absolutely no government approval whatsoever.
